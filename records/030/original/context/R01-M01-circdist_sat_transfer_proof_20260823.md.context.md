603	> **Remark 6.5 (what each boundary buys, quantitatively).** Drop the round-0 detector (noisy or
604	> unknown initialisation). Then (W) becomes `u(S_r) = H_Z σ_r + κ` for an unconstrained constant
605	> `κ ∈ F₂^ℬ`, and the correct object is no longer a single `(u,A,v)` but the family
606	> `A_κ[σ,σ'] := w_M(σ'−σ, H_Z σ + κ)` with `d_R = min_κ ( u ⊙ A_κ^{⊙R} ⊙ v )` — equivalently a
607	> state space `Σ × F₂^ℬ` in which `κ` is carried as a constant component. Drop the terminal detector
608	> instead and `v` loses its first clause, becoming `v[σ] = 0` iff `σ ≠ [0]`, which is a *different*
609	> and generally smaller quantity. **Both boundaries are load-bearing, in different places**, and
610	> this is the precise form of the round-0/terminal asymmetry.
