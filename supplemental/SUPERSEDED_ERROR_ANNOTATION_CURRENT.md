# Current annotation: Yoshida first-level categories

Use [the Yoshida first-level guide](annotations-yoshida65-level1-v1-20260922/README.md), [all 65 annotated records](annotations-yoshida65-level1-v1-20260922/RECORD_CLASSIFICATION.md), and [the full source/witness comparison](annotations-yoshida65-level1-v1-20260922/EVIDENCE_REVIEW.md).

The categories are problem understanding, mathematical concept knowledge, arithmetic/algebraic manipulation, logical/reasoning, and others. They follow Yoshida et al. (2026), Table 2 / Section 3.2, with research-context adaptations explicitly documented. No second-level labels, standalone proof-gap category, or new overclaim split are assigned.

Counts of unique records carrying each label: I=2, II=6, III=15, IV=42, V=2. There are 65 records and 67 assignments: 63 single-label records and two double-label records (022 and 037). These are corpus-description counts, not independent experiments or model error rates.

The earlier self-designed five-label, twenty-label, and exclusive-classification drafts are superseded; their historical files remain available. Decisions were made against the original source excerpts and scoped witnesses, not by renaming old labels.

The 65 accepted source/witness dossiers remain [the frozen v1.1 release](dataset-witness-repaired-v1.1-20260922/INDEX.md). Admission, source material, witness content, and manuscript text are unchanged.
