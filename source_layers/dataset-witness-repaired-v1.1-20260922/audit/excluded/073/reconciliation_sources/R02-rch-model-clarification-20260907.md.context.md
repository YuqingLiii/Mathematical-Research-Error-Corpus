25	The author identified the following exact diagnostic for the broader
26	orthogonal-action interpretation (not a refutation of GOC-T6's permutation
27	model). Set A=I_2, U=V=I_2, and let the nonidentity element of C_2 act
28	on both sides by
29	
30	    R=(1/sqrt(2)) [[1,1],[1,-1]].
31	
32	Then R^2=I and RA=AR, but R is not a permutation matrix. The positive
33	definite weight H=[[1,1/2],[1/2,1]] has unit diagonal and is feasible.
34	Direct multiplication gives RHR^T=diag(3/2,1/2). Hence the average
35	(H+RHR^T)/2 has diagonal (5/4,3/4), and is not feasible.
36	This exhibits the missing model boundary; it must not be hidden by saying
37	that general orthogonal equivariance alone permutes the diagonal.
