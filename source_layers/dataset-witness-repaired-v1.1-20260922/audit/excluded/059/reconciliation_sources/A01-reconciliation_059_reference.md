# Record 059: scope of the source-attribution allegation

The exact initial patch already states that the published display substituted a generic bound for R(P), and presents retaining the norm ratio as its own derivation. The later correction inaccurately paraphrases that initial attribution as if no such distinction were made.

The primary reference is [Chen, Garza-Vargas and van Handel, arXiv:2412.00593v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.00593v3), checked 2026-09-22. Section 7.6 uses the generic norm bound. Theorem 9.7 treats the disjoint tensor Gaussian setting, and the paragraph immediately following its proof explicitly allows the analogous classical compact-group ensembles. Hence those two observations alone do not refute what the original patch actually says.

This excludes the current misattribution witness, not certifies the proposed retained-radius tail estimate. A specific defective uniformity step would need its own claim-aligned argument; the separately bound erroneous epsilon estimate remains record 003. No new error is invented here to preserve the old count.
