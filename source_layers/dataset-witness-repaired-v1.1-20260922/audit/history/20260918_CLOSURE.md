audit of Rounds 43--58 did not contain enough earlier source material to
re-prove the imported baseline/Wiener necessity. That audit explicitly says:

- the problem is checkability **from that package alone**;
- no reversed implication was found; and
- the finding is a coverage issue, not a demonstrated counterexample.

coefficient identity

\[

\sqrt{D+1}|T_\nu|\le
\sum_{(x,y)\ne(\pm1,0)}|W_{\nu;x,y}|.

repeated raw frequencies are incorporated. The Round 36 local audit checks the

the concentrated audit of Rounds 24--42 marks the R36 lower-bound chain and the
R38 translated-cofactor Wiener domination as proved in their stated ansatz

Therefore M21 is resolved as:

## Search decision

Do not continue undirected searches for more cases before the v1 paper. That
would change the sampling frame after analysis has begun and make the corpus
harder to describe. Any later discovery should enter a separate prospective
`v1.1` or held-out extension ledger without changing the frozen v1 denominator.

The next work should be quality upgrading rather than case discovery:

1. independently replay the 51 M2 rows where the witness can be reproduced,
   prioritizing load-bearing and high-severity records; retaining M2 is
   acceptable when the historical source-bound witness is sufficient but not
   cheaply executable;
2. create leakage-free pre-error snapshots and reference answers;
3. publish inclusion/exclusion rules and the 90-entry candidate accounting;
4. separate error-conditioned analyses from any future model error-rate
   experiment, because this corpus has no denominator of all proof attempts.
