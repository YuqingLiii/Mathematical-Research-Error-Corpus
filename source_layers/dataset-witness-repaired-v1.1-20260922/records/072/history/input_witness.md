## Witness

A minimum over an empty feasible set is undefined unless an explicit extended-value convention is supplied.

### Argument or calculation

For A=diag(2,1), the top singular spaces have U=V=(1,0)^T. A scalar weight h gives diag(VhV^T)=(h,0), which cannot equal (1,1). There is no feasible weight and no spectral-bound-saturating vector strategy. The phrase 'the minimum real vector dimension ... is the minimum rank of a feasible H' therefore needs a nonemptiness qualification or a convention assigning both quantities +infinity.
