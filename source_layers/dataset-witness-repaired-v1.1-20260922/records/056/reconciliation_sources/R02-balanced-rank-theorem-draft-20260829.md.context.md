68	Suppose a finite group has transitive permutation actions `P_g` on the rows
69	and `Q_g` on the columns and
70	
71	```text
72	P_g A Q_g^T = A
73	```
74	
75	for every `g`.  The top left and right singular subspaces are invariant, and
76	their orthogonal projectors commute with the corresponding permutation
77	actions.  GO-T3 therefore supplies the two constant diagonals in GO-T2.  Thus
78	
79	```text
80	beta_vec(A) = sqrt(mn) ||A||_op.
81	```
82	
83	The proof also gives explicit extremizing vectors: the normalized rows of any
84	paired top left/right singular bases.  For a square group-orbit matrix this is
85	`beta_vec(A)=n||A||_op`.
